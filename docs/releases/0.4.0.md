# t4a-cuda-kernels 0.4.0

0.4.0 exists because the C ABI revision moved: `T4A_CUDA_ABI_REVISION` is 15. A catalogue pinned at 14 cannot be
swapped for this one at runtime, and a downstream `=0.3.0` pin has to look. (crates.io 0.3.0 carries ABI 14, the
same contract as 0.2.0.)

## What changed since 0.3.0

- **Layout-taking elementwise entries** (`cuda/src/elementwise.cu`): `t4a_cuda_strided_unary`,
  `t4a_cuda_strided_binary`, `t4a_cuda_strided_compare` and `t4a_cuda_strided_select`, plus the `_any` general-path
  twins. Every operand is read through its own layout (permuted, offset, stride-zero broadcast, negative stride), so a
  consumer no longer stages a view into a compact copy before an elementwise launch. New Rust surface:
  `Entry::{StridedUnary, StridedBinary, StridedCompare, StridedSelect}`, `StridedBinaryOp`,
  `plan_strided_unary`, `plan_strided_binary`, `plan_strided_compare`, `plan_strided_select`.
- **Scalar-side `ADD` / `SUB` on `t4a_cuda_binary`** (`BinaryOp::Add`, `BinaryOp::Sub`, tags 14 and 15), all six
  numeric dtypes, `ScalarSide::Left`/`Right` only.
- The flat binary and comparison bodies now call shared value helpers (`binary_float_value`, `binary_int_value`,
  `compare_value`) that the strided entries also call. Their formulas are unchanged.

## Numerical contract

A strided launch is bit-identical to the mirrored flat entry on compact copies of its operands, except for the sign and
payload of a NaN result, which the ABI leaves unspecified (operand-position NaN propagation in `FMUL`/`DMUL`/`DFMA`;
see `docs/abi/c-kernel-abi.md`, "Revision 15").

## What a consumer has to do

- Rebuild against revision 15; the revision check makes a stale pair refuse.
- Nothing existing was removed or renamed: `Entry::ALL` and `GeneralEntry::ALL` grew by four entries each, and
  `BinaryOp` grew by two variants (a non-exhaustive `match` over `Entry` or `BinaryOp` in a consumer must add arms).
